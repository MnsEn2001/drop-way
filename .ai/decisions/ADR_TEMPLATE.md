# .ai/decisions/ADR_TEMPLATE.md

Use this template to record an architectural decision.

## Title
Give the decision a short, descriptive title.

## Context
Describe the situation, constraint, or problem that prompted the decision.

## Decision
State the chosen approach clearly.

## Alternatives considered
List the main alternatives and why they were not chosen, if relevant.

## Consequences
Describe:
- What becomes easier
- What becomes harder
- Any risks, follow-up work, or limitations

## Status
Use one of:
- Proposed
- Accepted
- Deprecated
- Superseded

## Date
Record the date the decision was written or accepted.
