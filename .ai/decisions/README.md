# .ai/decisions/README.md

This directory is for recording architectural decisions that affect the project's direction or structure.

## When to record a decision
- When a non-obvious technical choice is made
- When there are multiple viable approaches and one is chosen
- When a constraint, convention, or tradeoff is important enough to preserve for later agents

## What to include
Use `.ai/decisions/ADR_TEMPLATE.md` and capture:
- Context
- Decision
- Alternatives considered
- Consequences
- Status
- Date

## What not to do
- Do not invent past decisions.
- Do not record obvious style preferences unless they materially affect the project.
- Do not duplicate information that already lives in `ARCHITECTURE.md` or `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` without a clear decision-focused reason.
